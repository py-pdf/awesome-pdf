Projects in this list must fulfill all of the following criteria:

1. **Maintained Projects** (REQ-1): Projects must be actively maintained to
   ensure they remain functional and secure. An indicator for active maintenance
   is that the last release is less than 12 months old and the last commit is
   less than 6 months old.
2. **Project Maturity** (REQ-2): AI makes it possible to quickly create new
   projects. Some of those projects are malicious, some are experimental, some
   still need to build a community around them which ensures long-term support.
   For this reason, **we require projects to be at least six months old**.
3. **Community** (REQ-3): A strong community ensures that the project receives
   ongoing support, contributions, and feedback. It's also a check to ensure
   malicious updates are caught. While we will not enforce strict requirements, a
   rule of thumb is **at least 5 contributors** and **at least 50 stars** on
   GitHub.
4. **Free Software Only** (REQ-4): Due to AI tools it has become very easy to
   create new software projects. It's not possible for us to check closed-source
   projects for potential malicious actors. FOSS projects which additionally
   have a commercial license are acceptable, as long as the source is publicly
   available and as long as non-commercial projects can use the software
   free of charge. This requirement especially means that a license MUST be given and clearly stated. It must be one of the [free licenses listed by GNU](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/license-list.en.html)

What will not be included in this list:
