Projects in this list must fulfill all of the following criteria:

1. **Maintained Projects** (REQ-1): Projects must be actively maintained to ensure they
   remain functional and secure. An indicator for active maintenance is that the
   last release is less than 12 months old and the last commit is less than
   6 months old.
2. **Project Maturity** (REQ-2): AI makes it possible to quickly create new projects.
  Some of those projects are malicious, some are experimental, some still need
  to build a community around them which ensures long-term support.
  For this reason, **we require projects to be at least six months old**.
3. **Community** (REQ-3): A strong community ensures that the project receives ongoing
  support, contributions, and feedback. It's also a check to ensure malicious
  updates are caught. While we will not enforce strict requirements, a rule of
  thumb is **at least 5 contributors** and **at least 50 stars** on GitHub.


What will not be included in this list:
